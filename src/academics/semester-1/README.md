# Semester 1

This is the Semester 1 index. The university scheme in force for your admission year decides the
course codes and syllabus — verify them with your department. No subject list is published here
until verified material for that subject is accepted.

## Published materials

**No verified student materials have been published for Semester 1 yet.**

This is deliberate: the library only lists files that the author wrote or explicitly allowed us
to share. An empty section is better than an unlicensed upload.
